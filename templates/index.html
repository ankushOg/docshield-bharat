  • Income Tax PAN Check: Validates 10-character structure, 4th-char entity code (P, C, H, F, A, T...), and matches 5th-char against Individual surname initial.
  • UIDAI Aadhaar Verhoeff Check: Enforces first-digit rule (2–9) and verifies the 12th digit via the official Dihedral D5 Verhoeff checksum algorithm.

  • RBI IFSC & India Post PIN: Verifies 11-char IFSC (5th char mandatory 0) and 6-digit Indian Postal Zone (1–8).
